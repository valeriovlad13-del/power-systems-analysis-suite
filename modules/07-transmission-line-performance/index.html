Core formula

Vs,phase ≈ Vr,phase + I(R + jX)

Worked example

For 50 MW at 132 kV and PF 0.9, I ≈ 243 A. Series voltage-drop approximation depends on both R and X.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a short-line approximate voltage-drop tool, not the full ABCD model. Long-line and nominal-π/T models require complex phasor calculations.

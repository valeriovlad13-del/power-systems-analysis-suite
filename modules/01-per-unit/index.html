Core formula

Zbase = (kVLL)² / MVA3φ; Ibase = MVA3φ / (√3 × kVLL)

Worked example

For a 100 MVA, 138 kV base, Zbase = 138²/100 = 190.44 Ω and Ibase = 100/(√3×138) = 0.4184 kA.

Assumptions and limitations

Keep voltage bases line-to-line and power bases three-phase when using these formulas. Convert equipment impedance bases only after confirming the voltage-base relationship.

Core formula

C(P) = a + bP + cP²; incremental cost = b + 2cP

Worked example

For C(P) = 100 + 8P + 0.02P² at 50 MW, C = 550 currency/h and incremental cost = 10 currency/MWh.

Assumptions and limitations

A single-unit cost evaluation is not an economic dispatch solution. Dispatch requires demand balance, unit limits, incremental-cost equalization, and loss treatment if applicable.

All coefficients must use the cost units specified beside the inputs.

Theory & formulas

Incremental cost and dispatch allocation

For quadratic cost Ci=ai+biPi+ciPi², incremental cost is dCi/dPi=bi+2ciPi.

Confirm the assumptions, sign convention, and units before applying a formula to a different system.

Practice problem

A generator has C(P) = 100 + 8P + 0.02P² currency/h. Find cost and incremental cost at 50 MW.

Check your result

C(50) = 100 + 8(50) + 0.02(50²) = 550 currency/h. Incremental cost = 8 + 2(0.02)(50) = 10 currency/MWh.

Scope and assumptions

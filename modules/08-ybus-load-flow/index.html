Core formula

Worked example

For Z = 0.1 + j0.4 Ω, y = 0.1 − j0.40.17 = 0.5882 − j2.3529 S.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a branch-admittance helper, not a complete Ybus or load-flow solver. A full solver must handle bus types, slack reference, PV reactive limits, convergence, and topology validation.

The per-unit values require both the stated MVA base and a consistent line-to-line voltage base.

Theory & formulas

Bus admittance and load-flow iteration

Ybus diagonal terms sum incident admittances; off-diagonal terms are the negative of direct branch admittance, absent special models.

Confirm the assumptions, sign convention, and units before applying a formula to a different system.

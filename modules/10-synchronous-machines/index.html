Core formula

Ns = 120f/P; Pmax = 3EV/Xs sinδ (phase RMS E and V in volts)

Worked example

A 4-pole machine on 60 Hz has synchronous speed Ns = 120×60/4 = 1800 rpm.

Assumptions and limitations

The power-angle relationship is an idealized classical model. It does not represent a complete transient-stability simulation or machine capability assessment.

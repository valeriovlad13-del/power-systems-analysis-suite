Core formula

S = P / PF; |Q| = P tan(cos⁻¹(PF))
Qc = P[tan(cos⁻¹(PFi)) − tan(cos⁻¹(PFt))]
Δ: Cphase = Qc/(3ωVLL²); Y: Cphase = Qc/(ωVLL²)

Worked example

At P = 100 kW and PF = 0.80, S = 125 kVA and |Q| = 75 kvar.

Assumptions and limitations

The sign of Q depends on whether the load is lagging or leading. Capacitor sizing must state connection (wye/delta), system voltage, frequency, and target power factor.
